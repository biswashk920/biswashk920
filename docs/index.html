About

I am a civil engineer from Nepal (B.Sc., Tribhuvan University, 2025), registered with the Nepal Engineering Council. My research is in hydrology and flood risk, mostly in Himalayan catchments.

I work at Laxmi Constructions and Consultancy Services on surveys, design and valuation, and I do research on the side, outside a university lab.

Research

I am first author on two published papers and one manuscript under review, and co-author on two more:

  • Flood susceptibility with deep learning, using Integrated Gradients to see which terrain, rainfall and land-use variables drive the result (Seti Gandaki basin).
  • Flash-flood susceptibility, from morphometric analysis, SCS-CN runoff and Snyder’s synthetic unit hydrograph (under review).
  • Urban flooding and low-impact development under climate scenarios, modelled in PCSWMM (Lamachaur, Pokhara).
  • Co-authored: avalanche susceptibility mapping with GIS-based AHP, and roadside drainage capacity.

Glaciers and glacial lakes are a newer interest. So far I have worked on inventory data and exploratory analysis, not on hazard modelling.

    Flood and watershed analysis

    • Flood_In_KaliGandaki: inundation risk mapping for the Kali Gandaki watershed using HAND
    • budi-gandaki-landslideFlood: event-based analysis of the causes of the 11 October 2026 Budhi Gandaki flooding
    • WaterShed_Screening_For_Hydropower_ROR: DEM-based screening of run-of-river hydropower reaches
    • watershed-delineation: Google Earth Engine and WhiteboxTools, from a DEM extent and an outlet point
    • Frequency_Analysis: Gumbel, Log-Pearson III, Log-Normal and GEV fits for rainfall and discharge
    • morphometric-pca-himalayan-basins: PCA of catchment morphometric parameters
    • Temperature_Reconstruction: filling an unknown station from neighbouring stations with XGBoost
    • WaterShed_Analysis and rainfall_analysis: supporting notebooks

    Glaciers and glacial lakes

    • Tsho_Rolpa_GLOF: glacial lake outburst flood analysis for Tsho Rolpa
    • Glacial-Lakes-Detection-CNN: an exploratory deep learning model for detecting glacial lakes. The results were not plausible, so it is unfinished and I am still working on it.
